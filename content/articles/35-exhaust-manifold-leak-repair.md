# Exhaust Manifold Leak: Why It Ticks Cold and Goes Quiet Hot

## EN

**An exhaust manifold leak sounds like a tick, a tap, or a soft puff that is
loudest in the first thirty seconds after a cold start and fades as the engine
warms.** That fade is the whole diagnosis. A lifter tick gets louder with heat; an
exhaust leak gets quieter, because the gap that makes the noise closes as the
casting expands.

The leak and the broken bolt are the same story. A cast iron manifold grows and
shrinks on every heat cycle. The bolts holding it do not. Over enough cycles the
gasket loses its crush and the outermost fasteners — the ones that see the most
movement — either back out or snap. Nearly every part below is sold to deal with one
half of that pair, plus the flange downstream that fails next. Prices were read on
live listings on 17 November 2026.

### Why the noise comes and goes

Exhaust gas escaping a gap makes noise because it is moving at high velocity
through a space too small for it. Cold, the manifold flange and the cylinder head
are at their smallest, the gap is widest, and the noise is at its worst. As the
engine reaches operating temperature the casting expands, the gap narrows, and the
tick drops away.

That is also why the problem seems to "fix itself" and then comes back a few months
later — the gap never closed, it just closed *enough*. Each cold start scrapes a
little more gasket material out of the joint.

What you hear depends on where the leak is. A failed gasket at the head flange ticks.
A crack in the casting itself is more often a hiss or a puff that follows engine
speed. A leak at the downpipe flange, downstream of the manifold, shows up as a drone
or a rasp rather than a tick.

### The three noises that get confused

Before buying anything, separate these, because only one of them is a manifold
job.

| What you hear | When it is loudest | What it usually is |
|---|---|---|
| Sharp tick, rhythmic, front of the engine bay | First 30 seconds, cold | **Manifold or gasket leak** |
| Tick that grows with heat and follows oil pressure | Warmed up, idle | Lifter or valvetrain |
| Soft flutter or puff under load | Steady throttle, accelerating | Cracked casting or failed flange |
| Whistle or rasp at cruise | Constant above a road speed | Exhaust joint downstream, not the manifold |

The cold-versus-hot behaviour is the fastest filter available and it costs nothing:
hood open, listen at the front wheel arch before the temperature gauge moves.

### How to confirm it in ten minutes

Tick aside, there are three checks that get repeated most often because they work
on a driveway without a smoke machine.

**The cold-soap test.** Mix dish soap and water in a spray bottle, start the engine
cold, and spray along the manifold-to-head joint within the first twenty seconds.
A leak blows bubbles. It stops working as the engine warms, which is exactly the
window you are looking for.

**The smoke test.** Exhaust smoke at the tailpipe on a cold start will drift from
a leaking joint before it drifts from anywhere else, particularly if the wind is
still. This is the cheapest version of what a shop does with a dedicated smoke
generator.

**The hose stethoscope.** A length of garden hose, one end to your ear and the
other moved slowly along the flange. Exhaust pulsation is unmistakable through it.
Keep the loose end away from the belts and the fan.

All three are done cold, on a vehicle that has not been run. A hot manifold will
burn you and will also have closed the gap you are trying to hear.

### The bolt that is already broken

On the engines this job is most common on, the leak is often accompanied by a bolt
head that is simply gone — not loose, not stripped, sheared off flush with the
head. The tick is the symptom and the missing fastener is the cause, which changes
what you should buy.

There are two honest routes, and on the parts list below they cost about the same.

**The clamp route.** A repair bracket bolts to an existing hole on the head, reaches
over the manifold flange, and presses it back against the head — no drilling and no
head removal. Kral's own listing describes the bracket as "a permanent fix that
allows you to repair exhaust leaks without removing cylinder heads or broken bolts,"
and makes the argument for itself in the same paragraph: the factory bolts are, in
its words, "too small," which is why the traditional repair "does not necessarily fix
the problem." Dorman's clamp listing makes the same case with less attribution.

**The removal route.** Drill the stub, extract it, replace the gasket, and torque new
hardware. It is more work, and it is the only route if more than one fastener is gone
or the flange is warped. A warped flange will not seal under a clamp, and no clamp
listing here claims it will.

The one thing a clamp cannot do for you is fit the wrong side. Kral's KAP169 listing
covers "passenger side front most bolt or driver's side rear most bolt position," and
then adds a note that is easy to miss until the part is in your hand: "The oil
dipstick tube will be on the passenger side of your vehicle when using this part. If
your engine oil dipstick tube is located on the driver's side of your vehicle, please
use part KAP485362DR." Two kits, chosen by where the dipstick sits — which is a
sixty-second look under the hood and not a guess. The driver's-side kit is **$39.90**
and scores 3.25 across three reviews, which makes it the weakest-rated bracket here;
read those three before you order rather than after.

### The flange that starts leaking next

The joint where the manifold meets the downpipe is the second failure point, and it
fails for an unglamorous reason: two steel flanges clamped together with a gasket
between them, flexing every time the engine moves on its mounts. Once the gasket
crushes out, the nuts come loose and the joint starts to rasp.

Repair flanges are cheap and they are sold by pipe diameter, not by vehicle, which
is why they are worth knowing about:

### Is any of this legal in California?

The rule that matters here is not a noise statute, because a repair does not change
how loud the truck is. It is [Cal. Veh. Code § 27156](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=27156),
which says in subsection (b) that a vehicle required to be equipped with a pollution
control device must be equipped with that device "correctly installed and in
operating condition," and adds:

> "No person shall disconnect, modify, or alter any such required device."

Subsection (c) extends that to selling or installing a part intended for use as part
of such a device "that alters or modifies the original design or performance." None
of the parts on this page is a performance part: every one replaces a failed original
component with a like-for-like one, which is restorative rather than modifying. That
is the distinction the statute draws, and it is the reason this page quotes it rather
than a noise rule.

That is the principle. Here is what the listings themselves say, which is what you
actually have to work with at the counter:

**No part on this page carries a CARB Executive Order number.** Not the gaskets, not
the clamps, not the manifolds, not the bolts. An EO number is what an *add-on or
modified* part carries after CARB has evaluated it; a like-for-like replacement part
does not go through that program, so the absence of an EO is not by itself a
statement that a part is illegal — but it does mean you cannot point at a number to
prove the part is exempt.

**Summit Racing carries a blanket notice on its product pages**: "Some parts are not
legal for use in California or other states with similar laws / regulations." That
notice appears site-wide, so it is not a statement about any one part in particular.

**The one listing here that names California does so to exclude it.** The Dorman
674-732 manifold's fitment note reads "Except California emissions. With air pump."
If your truck has California emissions equipment, that is the listing telling you it
is the wrong part for you.

What that adds up to practically: a factory-style gasket, a factory-style manifold
and a repair clamp that restores the original seal are the safe category for a
California truck, and an emissions-field note that says "except California" is a hard
no. If the part you want has neither an EO number nor a matching emissions note, ask
the seller for the exemption status in writing before you install it — a visual
inspection at smog time is where this gets caught, and California's Smog Check
program [explicitly includes a visual inspection of the emissions control
system](https://www.bar.ca.gov/consumer/smog-check-program) alongside the functional
and OBD checks.

### One reason to fix it that is not about noise

An exhaust leak ahead of the catalytic converter is upstream of everything that
normally cleans the exhaust, and the cabin air intake sits in the same engine bay.
Carbon monoxide is why that matters: the CDC describes it as "an odorless, colorless
gas that can cause sudden illness and death if inhaled," and reports that each year
more than 400 Americans die from unintentional CO poisoning not linked to fires. An
exhaust smell in the cabin at a stop light is the symptom to act on, not the tick.

### What to do first

Start it cold, listen at the front wheel arch before the temperature gauge moves, and
note whether the tick fades. If it does, spray soapy water along the head flange in
that first half-minute. Bubbles plus intact fasteners means a **$17.99** to **$23.99**
gasket. A missing bolt head means pricing the clamp for that specific position and an
extractor set as a fallback before deciding whether this is a driveway job or a shop
job.

**찬 상태 비눗물 테스트.** 주방세제와 물을 스프레이병에 넣고, 냉간 시동 후 20초

### 캘리포니아에서는 합법인가

여기서 적용되는 규정은 소음 기준이 아닙니다. 수리는 차를 더 시끄럽게 만들지
않기 때문입니다. [Cal. Veh. Code § 27156](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=27156)
(b)항은 오염방지장치가 장착된 차량은 그 장치가 "올바르게 장착되고 작동 상태"여야
한다고 규정하고, 이어서 이렇게 덧붙입니다.

> "No person shall disconnect, modify, or alter any such required device."

(c)항은 그러한 장치의 "원래 설계나 성능을 변경하는" 부품의 판매·설치까지
확장합니다. 이 페이지의 부품은 모두 성능 부품이 아니라 고장 난 원래 부품의
교체품입니다.

실무에서 확인할 것은 다음 세 가지입니다.

**이 페이지의 어떤 부품에도 CARB EO 번호가 없습니다.** EO 번호는 CARB가 평가한
애드온·개조 부품에 붙는 것이고, 동일 사양 교체 부품은 그 프로그램을 거치지
않습니다. 따라서 EO가 없다는 것 자체가 불법이라는 뜻은 아니지만, 면제를 번호로
증명할 수는 없다는 뜻입니다.

**Summit Racing은 제품 페이지에 공통 안내문을 표시합니다.** "일부 부품은
캘리포니아 또는 유사 법규가 있는 주에서 사용이 합법적이지 않습니다"라는 문구이며,
사이트 전체에 적용되므로 특정 부품에 대한 판단은 아닙니다.

**캘리포니아를 명시한 유일한 리스팅은 배제를 위해 명시합니다.** Dorman 674-732의
핏먼트 표기는 "캘리포니아 배기가스 제외. 에어 펌프 포함"입니다.

정리하면, 공장형 가스켓·매니폴드·원래 밀봉을 복원하는 수리 클램프가 캘리포니아
차량에 안전한 범주이고, 배기가스 항목에 "캘리포니아 제외"라고 적힌 부품은
명확한 제외입니다. EO 번호도, 배기가스 표기도 없는 부품이라면 판매처에 면제
여부를 서면으로 확인하십시오. 캘리포니아 Smog Check는 [배기가스 제어장치의 육안
검사](https://www.bar.ca.gov/consumer/smog-check-program)를 기능 검사·OBD 검사와
함께 포함합니다.

### 소음이 아닌 다른 이유

## ES

**Una fuga en el múltiple de escape suena como un tic o un soplo que es más fuerte
en los primeros treinta segundos tras un arranque en frío y se apaga cuando el motor
calienta.** Ese desvanecimiento es el diagnóstico. Un taqué se vuelve más ruidoso con
el calor; una fuga de escape se vuelve más silenciosa, porque la holgura que hace el
ruido se cierra al dilatarse la fundición.

La fuga y el tornillo roto son la misma historia. Un múltiple de hierro fundido crece
y se contrae en cada ciclo térmico; los tornillos que lo sujetan no. Con suficientes
ciclos la junta pierde su aplastamiento y los sujetadores de los extremos —los que más
se mueven— se aflojan o se parten. Los precios de esta página se leyeron en anuncios
reales el 17 de noviembre de 2026.

### Por qué el ruido va y viene

El gas de escape escapa por una rendija y hace ruido porque se mueve a gran velocidad
por un espacio demasiado pequeño. En frío, la brida del múltiple y la culata están en
su punto más pequeño, la holgura es máxima y el ruido también. Al alcanzar la
temperatura de trabajo la fundición se dilata, la holgura se estrecha y el tic baja.

Por eso parece "arreglarse solo" y vuelve meses después: la holgura nunca se cerró,
solo se cerró *lo suficiente*. Cada arranque en frío arranca un poco más de material
de la junta.

### Tres ruidos que se confunden

| Qué se oye | Cuándo es más fuerte | Qué suele ser |
|---|---|---|
| Tic agudo y rítmico, parte delantera | Primeros 30 s en frío | **Fuga de múltiple o junta** |
| Tic que crece con el calor | En caliente, al ralentí | Taqué o distribución |
| Soplido suave bajo carga | Acelerando | Fundición agrietada o brida |
| Silbido áspero en crucero | Velocidad constante | Unión aguas abajo, no el múltiple |

La diferencia entre frío y caliente es el filtro más rápido y no cuesta nada.

### Confirmarlo en diez minutos

**Prueba de agua jabonosa en frío.** Mezcla lavavajillas y agua, arranca en frío y
rocía a lo largo de la unión con la culata en los primeros veinte segundos. Una fuga
hace burbujas. Deja de funcionar al calentarse el motor, que es justo la ventana que
buscas.

**Prueba de humo.** El humo del escape en un arranque en frío sale primero por la
unión con fuga. Es la versión barata de lo que hace un taller con un generador de humo.

**Estetoscopio de manguera.** Una manguera de jardín, un extremo al oído y el otro
recorriendo la brida despacio. La pulsación del escape es inconfundible.

Las tres se hacen con el motor frío. Un múltiple caliente quema y además ya cerró la
holgura que intentas oír.

### El tornillo que ya está roto

En estos motores la fuga suele venir acompañada de una cabeza de tornillo que
sencillamente no está: no floja, no redondeada, sino cortada a ras de la culata.

**La ruta de la abrazadera.** Un soporte de reparación se atornilla a un orificio
existente en la culata, alcanza la brida del múltiple y la presiona contra la culata.
No hay que taladrar ni quitar la culata.

**La ruta del desmontaje.** Taladrar el resto, extraerlo, cambiar la junta y apretar
herrajes nuevos. Es más trabajo y es la única ruta si falta más de un sujetador o si
la brida está deformada. Una brida deformada no sella con abrazadera.

### ¿Es legal en California?

La norma aplicable no es la de ruido, porque una reparación no hace más ruidoso el
vehículo. Es el
[Cal. Veh. Code § 27156](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=27156),
que en el apartado (b) exige que el dispositivo de control de emisiones esté
"correctamente instalado y en condiciones de funcionamiento" y añade:

> "No person shall disconnect, modify, or alter any such required device."

El apartado (c) lo extiende a vender o instalar una pieza que "altere o modifique el
diseño o el rendimiento original". Ninguna pieza de esta página es una pieza de
rendimiento: todas sustituyen un componente original que falló.

Lo que dicen los propios anuncios, que es con lo que uno trabaja en el mostrador:

**Ninguna pieza de esta página lleva número CARB Executive Order.** El EO es lo que
lleva una pieza *adicional o modificada* tras la evaluación de CARB; una pieza de
sustitución equivalente no pasa por ese programa, así que su ausencia no equivale a
ilegalidad, pero tampoco permite demostrar la exención con un número.

**Summit Racing muestra un aviso general** en sus páginas de producto: "Algunas piezas
no son legales para su uso en California u otros estados con leyes similares". Aparece
en todo el sitio, así que no es un juicio sobre una pieza concreta.

**El único anuncio que nombra California es para excluirla.** La nota del Dorman
674-732 dice "Excepto emisiones de California. Con bomba de aire".

En la práctica: una junta, un múltiple y una abrazadera de estilo original que
restauren el sellado de fábrica son la categoría segura, y una nota de emisiones que
diga "excepto California" es un no rotundo. California incluye una
[inspección visual del sistema de control de emisiones](https://www.bar.ca.gov/consumer/smog-check-program)
en su Smog Check, junto a las pruebas funcional y OBD.

### Otra razón que no es el ruido

Una fuga antes del catalizador está aguas arriba de todo lo que normalmente limpia el
escape, y la entrada de aire del habitáculo está en el mismo vano motor. Los CDC
describen el monóxido de carbono como "un gas inodoro e incoloro que puede causar
enfermedad repentina y muerte si se inhala", y estiman más de 400 muertes anuales por
intoxicación no intencional no vinculada a incendios. Si huele a escape dentro del
habitáculo detenido en un semáforo, ese olor es la señal.

### Qué hacer primero

Arranca en frío, escucha en el paso de rueda delantero antes de que se mueva el
indicador de temperatura y fíjate si el tic disminuye. Si disminuye, rocía agua
jabonosa en la brida durante esos primeros treinta segundos. Si hay burbujas y los
sujetadores están intactos, la junta cuesta entre **$17.99** y **$23.99**. Si falta
una cabeza de tornillo, presupuesta la abrazadera de esa posición concreta y el juego
extractor antes de decidir si es trabajo de garaje o de taller.
