# One of Five Truck Programmers Publishes a CARB EO Number

## EN

**Five handheld programmers, $268.95 to $448.95, and one of them tells you
whether it is legal in California.** Not one of the cheapest four, either — the
only listing that publishes a CARB Executive Order number is tied for the most
expensive in the group.

The rest publish no emissions line of any kind. That is the finding, and it is
worth being precise about what it does and does not mean.

**Every price, rating and emissions field below was read from the product spec
block on 19 November 2026.**

### The five, in price order

| Device | Price | Rating | Publish an EO? | What it writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superchips GM **Flashcal** 2545 | **$268.95** | 4.0 · 18 | **No EO field** | Speedometer, gears, TPMS — **no tune** |
| SCT **X4** Power Flash 7015 | **$377.99** | 4.25 · 79 | **No EO field** | **Pre-loaded** tune files |
| Bully Dog **BDX** 40470 | **$377.99** | 3.5 · 16 | **No EO field** | Pre-loaded, 10 custom tunes |
| Superchips **Flashpaq F5 Pro** 3874 | **$448.95** | 4.5 · 8 | **No EO field** | **No pre-loaded tunes** |
| DiabloSport **inTune i3** 8200 | **$448.95** | 4.5 · 18 | **Yes — D-770, D-802-19** | Pre-loaded, 50-state certified |

### What the one EO listing actually says

The DiabloSport spec block contains two fields the other four do not have at all:

- **CARB EO Number(s):** D-770, D-802-19
- **Emissions:** California EO Approved

Two numbers, not one, because D-802-19 is the later amendment superseding parts
of D-770. Both are published together, which is the correct way to list them —
quoting only D-770 would leave out coverage the device actually has.

**D-770 is titled, in full: *"Relating to Exemptions Under Section 27156 of the
California Vehicle Code — DiabloSport inTune 12 CE, inTune 13 CE, inTune 12,
inTune 13, Predator 2, and Trinity 2 Programmers."*** Note what that list does
and does not contain: the i3 is not named in the D-770 title as I read it, which
is exactly why the second number matters. The later order covers the GM V-8 tunes
manufactured by Powerteq — DiabloSport's parent, alongside Edge Products and
Superchips.

**Check both numbers against your model year, not just the product page.** An EO
covers specific vehicles and specific model years, and a listing that names the
EO does not tell you your truck is inside that coverage.

### The four that publish nothing

I searched each of the other four pages for **CARB**, **Executive Order**,
**EO#**, **50-State**, and **emissions**. All five searches came back empty on all
four listings.

What that means, stated carefully: **the listing does not publish an EO, so you
cannot confirm from it that the device is legal in California.** It does not prove
no EO exists — a manufacturer can hold one and the retailer not print it. But you
cannot buy on the strength of a field that is not there, and the practical result
is the same for a California buyer: unverifiable until you go find the order.

**A part with no applicable CARB Executive Order is not legal for street use on
a registered vehicle in California and will fail a smog visual inspection.** That
is the rule the field exists to answer, and on four of these five listings the
field is absent.

### The one explicit warning, and what it is not

The Bully Dog BDX carries a **Special Attention** line: *"Part not for use on
applications with deleted emission controls."* That is the most direct emissions
statement of the four, and it is a restriction rather than a compliance claim —
it tells you what the device must not be paired with, not that it is approved.

Separately, all five listings display a **CA Warning** badge. That badge is
Proposition 65 — the panel behind it reads *"Cancer and Reproductive Harm."* **It
has nothing to do with CARB and nothing to do with emissions.** Almost every part
on the site carries it, including brake rotors. Do not read it as an emissions
verdict in either direction.

### The cheapest device does not tune

The **Flashcal at $268.95** is the least expensive of the five, and it does not
write a performance calibration at all. Its listed functions are:

- Speedometer correction for tires **26 in. to 38 in.**
- Recalibration for **gear and axle swaps**
- Speed and rev limiter options
- **TPMS** options
- Read and clear diagnostic trouble codes
- Battery voltage display, real-time data logging and monitoring

That is a **recalibrator**, not a tuner. It fixes the things a lift and bigger
tires break — the speedometer, the shift points, the TPMS thresholds — and leaves
the engine calibration alone. For a truck on 35s it is the device most owners
actually need, and it is the cheapest.

It is also upgradeable to a Flashpaq, per the listing, which matters if you want
both jobs from one purchase.

### "Pro" costs more and contains fewer tunes

This is the detail that should stop you at the checkout page:

- **SCT X4, $377.99** — *"pre-loaded with dyno-proven tune files for select
  vehicles."*
- **Superchips Flashpaq F5 Pro, $448.95** — *"there are no pre-loaded tunes."*

The F5 Pro's actual job is to **read stock files and load custom-tuned files built
through HP Tuners software by a dealer.** It holds four custom tunes and logs up to
eight PIDs. If you do not have an HP Tuners dealer and a custom tune to load, the
$448.95 device gives you nothing the $377.99 one does not.

**"Pro" in this product line means unlocked for custom files, not bundled with
more.** That is a legitimate product — if you are working with a tuner, it is the
one to buy — but it is not an upgrade path most buyers think they are on.

### Two things that are not in the price

- **The F5 Pro needs a tuner in the loop.** The device is the hardware half; the
  tune is a separate purchase from a dealer.
- **The SCT X4 lists a PC requirement**: *"Requires use of Windows Vista, Windows
  7, 8, or 10 for updates."* No Mac path is stated. If you update on a Mac, plan
  around it.

### What to check before buying any of these

1. **Does the listing publish a CARB EO number?** If not, you cannot confirm
   legality from the page.
2. **Does the EO cover your model year?** Read the order, not the product title.
3. **Are there pre-loaded tunes**, or does the device need a dealer-supplied file?
4. **Do you need a tune at all**, or just a speedometer correction for bigger
   tires? The cheapest device here does the second job and not the first.
5. **What does it monitor and log?** PIDs and custom-tune slots differ per device
   and are listed.

### What I could not verify

- **Whether the four without a published EO actually hold one.** Absence from a
  listing is not proof of absence. I did not locate an order for any of them.
- **The full vehicle coverage behind either DiabloSport EO.** I read the D-770
  title and a summary of D-802-19, not the orders' complete application tables.
  CARB's searchable EO database was not reachable during this research.
- **Horsepower or torque gains for any device.** None of the five publishes a dyno
  figure for a specific application on the listing, and I will not repeat a
  manufacturer claim as a measurement.
- **Fitment beyond the makes named in each summary.** Application tables load
  separately and were not read. Confirm by year, make, model and engine.
- **Warranty effect.** Not covered here; see the separate tuning guide for how
  Magnuson-Moss works in practice.

### EO를 공개한 유일한 제품

DiabloSport 스펙란에는 나머지 네 개에 없는 항목이 두 개 있습니다.
**CARB EO 번호: D-770, D-802-19**, 그리고 **배기가스: California EO Approved**입니다.

D-802-19는 D-770의 일부를 대체하는 이후 개정이므로 두 번호를 함께 적는 것이
정확합니다. **D-770은 캘리포니아 차량법 27156조에 따른 면제에 관한 문서**이고,
i3는 그 제목에 이름이 없으므로 두 번째 번호가 중요합니다. 제품 페이지가 아니라
**EO가 커버하는 모델 연식을 확인해야 합니다.**

### EO를 공개하지 않는 네 제품

네 페이지에서 **CARB, Executive Order, EO#, 50-State, emissions**를 모두
검색했고 전부 나오지 않았습니다. 이는 **목록만으로는 캘리포니아 합법 여부를 확인할
수 없다**는 뜻입니다. EO가 없다는 증명은 아니지만, 없는 항목을 근거로 살 수는
없습니다. **적용 가능한 CARB EO가 없는 부품은 캘리포니아 등록 차량에 사용할 수
없고 스모그 육안 검사에서 탈락합니다.**

### CA Warning은 CARB가 아닙니다

다섯 제품 모두 **CA Warning** 배지가 붙어 있는데, 이는 **제안 65(발암 및 생식
독성)** 표시입니다. 배기가스와 무관하며 브레이크 로터에도 붙습니다.

### 가장 싼 제품은 튠을 하지 않습니다

**Flashcal $268.95**는 성능 캘리브레이션을 쓰지 않습니다. 타이어 **26~38인치**
속도계 보정, 기어·액슬 교체 보정, TPMS, 속도·회전 한계, DTC 삭제, 데이터
로깅입니다. 리프트와 큰 타이어로 깨진 것들을 고치는 장비이고, 엔진 캘리브레이션은
건드리지 않습니다. 35인치 트럭에 실제로 필요한 것이 이것이며 가장 쌉니다.

### 'Pro'는 더 비싸고 튠은 더 적습니다

**SCT X4 $377.99**는 다이나 검증 튠 파일이 기본 탑재입니다. **F5 Pro $448.95**는
*"기본 탑재 튠이 없습니다"*라고 명시합니다. HP Tuners 딜러가 만든 커스텀 파일을
넣는 장비로, 딜러와 커스텀 튠이 없다면 $71 더 비싼 값을 할 이유가 없습니다.

### 확인하지 못한 것

네 제품의 EO 보유 여부, 두 EO의 전체 적용 차량, 마력·토크 수치, 연식별 적합성은
확인하지 못했습니다. CARB의 EO 검색 데이터베이스는 이번 조사 중 접속되지 않았습니다.

## ES

**Cinco programadores portátiles, de $268,95 a $448,95, y solo uno indica si es
legal en California.** No es uno de los cuatro más baratos: el único que publica
un número de Orden Ejecutiva de CARB cuesta lo mismo que el más caro.

Los otros cuatro no publican ninguna línea de emisiones.

Todos los precios y campos se leyeron el **19 de noviembre de 2026**.

### Los cinco

| Dispositivo | Precio | Valoración | ¿Publica EO? | Qué escribe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superchips Flashcal 2545 | **$268,95** | 4,0 · 18 | **No** | Velocímetro, relaciones, TPMS — **sin programación** |
| SCT X4 7015 | **$377,99** | 4,25 · 79 | **No** | Archivos **precargados** |
| Bully Dog BDX 40470 | **$377,99** | 3,5 · 16 | **No** | Precargado, 10 personalizadas |
| Superchips Flashpaq F5 Pro 3874 | **$448,95** | 4,5 · 8 | **No** | **Sin archivos precargados** |
| DiabloSport inTune i3 8200 | **$448,95** | 4,5 · 18 | **Sí — D-770, D-802-19** | Precargado, certificado 50 estados |

### El único que publica su EO

La ficha del DiabloSport tiene dos campos que los otros cuatro no tienen:
**CARB EO Number(s): D-770, D-802-19** y **Emissions: California EO Approved**.

Se publican los dos números porque D-802-19 es una enmienda posterior que sustituye
parte de D-770. **D-770 se titula «Relating to Exemptions Under Section 27156 of
the California Vehicle Code»** y en ese título no aparece el i3, que es justamente
por lo que el segundo número importa. Hay que comprobar el año del vehículo contra
la orden, no contra la página del producto.

### Los cuatro que no publican nada

Busqué **CARB**, **Executive Order**, **EO#**, **50-State** y **emissions** en las
cuatro fichas: ninguna devolvió resultado. Eso significa que **la ficha no permite
confirmar la legalidad en California**. No demuestra que no exista una orden, pero
no se puede comprar amparándose en un campo ausente.

**Una pieza sin Orden Ejecutiva de CARB aplicable no es legal para uso en calle en
California y no pasará la inspección visual.**

### El aviso CA no es CARB

Las cinco fichas muestran una insignia **CA Warning** que corresponde a la
Proposición 65 —«cáncer y daño reproductivo»— y no tiene relación con emisiones.

### El más barato no programa nada

El **Flashcal de $268,95** no escribe calibración de rendimiento: corrige el
velocímetro para neumáticos de **26 a 38 pulgadas**, relaciones y ejes, TPMS, y lee
códigos. Es un recalibrador, y es lo que de verdad necesita un camión con
neumáticos grandes.

### «Pro» cuesta más y trae menos

El **SCT X4, $377,99**, llega con archivos precargados. El **F5 Pro, $448,95**,
dice literalmente que **no trae archivos precargados**: sirve para cargar archivos
personalizados hechos por un distribuidor de HP Tuners. Sin ese distribuidor, los
$71 extra no compran nada.

### Lo que no pude verificar

No verifiqué si los cuatro restantes tienen una orden, la cobertura completa de
ninguna de las dos órdenes, cifras de potencia ni ajuste por año y motor. La base
de datos de órdenes de CARB no fue accesible durante esta investigación.
