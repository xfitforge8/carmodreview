Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information CarModReview (“we”, “us”) collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you contact us by email or submit a form, we receive what you send — typically your name, email address, and message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools record standard technical data: IP address (often truncated or anonymized), browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent. This is aggregate data used to understand traffic and improve the site.

Cookies. We use cookies and similar technologies for three purposes: - Essential — site functionality, security, remembering preferences. - Analytics — understanding which pages are useful (e.g., Google Analytics).

Affiliate and third-party cookies

When you click an affiliate link, the merchant or affiliate network may set a cookie on your device to attribute the eventual sale. These cookies typically expire within 24 hours to 30 days depending on the program. We do not control these cookies and do not receive your personal data from them — we receive aggregate commission reports only.

Third parties that may set cookies through this site include Amazon, eBay, CJ Affiliate, Impact, Rakuten, Awin, ShareASale, Google Analytics, and individual retailers.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve the site
  • To respond to inquiries
  • To measure aggregate traffic and page performance
  • To attribute affiliate commissions
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your email address with advertisers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

EU/UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR): Our lawful bases for processing are legitimate interest (site operation, analytics) and consent (non-essential cookies). You may withdraw consent at any time through the cookie banner or your browser settings.

South Korea (PIPA): You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@carmodreview.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data deletion (Facebook and Instagram)

This section covers data relating to Facebook and Instagram specifically, and is the deletion instruction we give to Meta as the operator of this app.

What the app does. Car Mod Review uses Meta’s APIs for one purpose: to publish our own posts to our own Instagram account. It does not ask you to log in with Facebook or Instagram, it has no user accounts, and it does not read, collect or store information about your Facebook or Instagram account. There is no profile for you to delete, because none is created.

What is still held. Meta records the fact that a post was published, exactly as it would for anything posted from the Instagram app, and that record is governed by Meta’s own privacy policy rather than this one. Separately, our web host and analytics keep the ordinary technical logs described above — IP address, browser, pages visited — which are not linked to any Facebook or Instagram identity.

How to request deletion. Email contact@carmodreview.com with the subject line data deletion. Include the Facebook or Instagram username the request relates to, and the address you want the reply sent to. We will:

  1. Confirm receipt within 7 days.
  2. Delete any data we hold that relates to that account. In practice this normally means nothing, because we hold none — we will tell you that explicitly rather than leaving you to assume it.
  3. Confirm completion in writing within 30 days.

If the request concerns a comment or message you left on one of our posts, deleting it at Meta removes it from our view as well; we do not keep copies of comments or messages once they are deleted at Meta.

Requests we cannot action. We cannot delete data held by Meta or by another platform, because we do not control it — use Meta’s own deletion tools for that. We will tell you which of the two a request falls under.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may break site features. Most browsers also offer a private/incognito mode that limits cookie persistence.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We retain inquiry emails only as long as needed to resolve the matter. We use HTTPS across the site and take reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no transmission over the internet is completely secure.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted with a revised “last updated” date at the top.
