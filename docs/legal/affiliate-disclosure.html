Affiliate Disclosure

Our current status

CarModReview does not currently have an affiliate relationship with any retailer. Some pages link to retailers so you can see the product we are describing. Those are ordinary links: we earn nothing from them, and they carry no tracking tag.

This page describes our policy in full, so that when a paid relationship does begin you already know the rules we operate under. The date at the top of this page changes when the status above changes.

If and when we join a program

If we are approved into an affiliate program, we will update the status above, and from that point on:

  • Links to that retailer may carry an affiliate tag, and we may earn a commission if you buy through them.
  • You never pay more. The price is the same whether you arrive through our link or not. Where a program offers a discount, we will say so on the page rather than presenting it as a feature of the product.
  • Commission does not buy a recommendation. We do not accept payment for a positive review, a particular rating, or a guaranteed position in a ranking. Commission rate is not an input to how we order products.
  • We mark affiliate links. A page carrying them shows a disclosure above the first link, not only here.
  • Some things we recommend earn us nothing. Some products we rate highly have no affiliate program at all. If a product with a generous commission is one we would not buy, we say that instead of recommending it.

Sponsored content

No post on this site is currently sponsored or paid for. If that changes, the fact will be disclosed prominently at the top of the article, above the content, regardless of the amount involved.

Editorial independence

Our editorial decisions — what to cover, how to assess it, and what to recommend — are made independently of any commercial relationship. Where we cannot verify a claim, we say so instead of filling the gap with confident-sounding prose. Where we have not personally tested a product, the page identifies the assessment as documentation-based rather than first-hand.

If you believe a recommendation here is inaccurate or commercially motivated, tell us. We will investigate and correct the record publicly where the correction is warranted.

Photography

Photographs on this site are licensed stock images, not pictures of our own vehicles.

An image illustrates a component or a setting. It is never presented as evidence of a test we performed. Where a guide describes how a part behaves, that conclusion comes from published specifications and documentation, and the page says so.
