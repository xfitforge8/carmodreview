What this is

Our guides compare parts by reading what owners actually wrote, not by repeating the listing copy. This page is the corpus those comparisons come from: 26 distinct products across 11 buying guides, carrying 8,156 individual owner ratings. The same records appear in the guides, quoted and dated.

What the numbers say

Across the 26 products with a published star distribution, 83.6% of all ratings are five stars. That is not a sign the parts are excellent. It is what retailer review distributions look like: people who bothered to review a part they chose and paid for tend to be pleased with it. The low-star reviews are the useful ones, which is why our guides quote those and ignore the four-star "great product, fast shipping" ones.

What this table does not tell you

The star average and the rating count are what the retailer publishes. We did not collect these reviews, we did not verify that the reviewers owned the part, and we cannot detect a manipulated review set. The install time is the listing's own claim, reproduced as written rather than normalised, and a guide will always tell you where owners disagreed with it.

Citing this

If you want to reference these figures, cite the page and the collection date: Car Mod Review, The dataset behind our guides, collected 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-11, https://carmodreview.com/data.html. The table is regenerated from the same source files the guides read, so a later version may carry a different collection date and different counts.
