Read this before you apply

Approval is not the bottleneck and neither is the application form. Programs approve sites that already send buyers. At six visits, an affiliate manager has nothing to judge, so several will decline or simply not reply — that is normal and it is not a rejection of the site.

File the applications anyway. Approval takes one to three weeks, and you want it sitting there before traffic arrives, not applied for on the day it does. Just don't expect an approval to produce income on its own.

The bigger finding: all 30 product links on the site point at americantrucks.com, and AmericanTrucks has no public affiliate program. If a reader buys today, you earn nothing. That single fact is worth more than every other row on this page.

Do it in this order

  1. One Impact account.

    Create the free partner account first. RealTruck and Summit Racing both run on Impact, so this is one form that unlocks two of the three strongest programs.

  2. Apply to RealTruck, then Summit Racing.

    RealTruck first because the 30-day window is four times Summit's 7-day one, and truck accessories suit the existing guides better. In the application, name carmodreview.com and point at the Silverado levelling-kit and cold-air-intake guides — those are the two with real photographs and product tables.

  3. Email AmericanTrucks and ask directly.

    There is no public program page, but every product link on the site goes to them, so a working relationship here converts existing traffic with no rewriting. Ask whether AmericanTrucks.com is covered under the RealTruck Impact campaign — Turn5 and RealTruck have been connected for a few years, and if they share a campaign this becomes a single approval. I could not verify that link, so ask rather than assume.

  4. Hold Amazon until traffic exists — not for the rate.

    At 4.50% on automotive it beats Summit Racing on hardware and covers the consumables no parts retailer sells. The reason to wait is the clock: a new Associates account gets 180 days from applying to refer three sales, and failing that test closes the door. Applying at six visits a month is a near-certain fail. Apply the month the site starts getting real visits.

  5. Re-check the dead links in a month.

    Program pages move often. The ones below all failed today and may well be back.
